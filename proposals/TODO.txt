Current protocol shape:

- Server FN-DSA key IDs are `key_id`, with `short_key_id` as the first 20
  base64url characters of `key_id`.
- Server `key_id` is minting-bound and SHA3-256-derived as defined by 00E4.
- Minting PoW is produced by the origin when the key body is generated. It is
  not a notary-issued challenge and not repeated for every notary acquisition.
- Notary provenance is a detached asset that notaries keep and redistribute
  with observations when available.
- `key_id_sha256`, `short_id`, and publication/notary challenge flows are
  obsolete for the live docs.
